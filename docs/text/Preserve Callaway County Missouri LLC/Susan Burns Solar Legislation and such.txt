POLITICAL  SUBDIVISIONS  (Section  67.5360) 

The  act  provides  that  the  governing  body  of  any  political 

subdivision  shall  not  approve  a  plan  relating  to  the  development 

of  the  political  subdivision  with  a  developer  whose  more  than 

half  of  the  solar  panels  or  the  solar  panel  components  are 

manufactured  or  sourced  by  an  individual  or  government  identified 

as  a  foreign  adversary.

Beginning  January  1,  2027,  An  owner  or  operator  of  a  solar

An  owner  or  operator  shall  maintain  a  solar  farm  until 

decommissioning.  Maintenance  shall  include  cosmetic  changes, 

structural  repairs,  and  maintenance  of  security  measures.  Any 

retrofit,  replacement,  or  refurbishment  of  equipment  shall  be  in

An  owner  or  operator  of  a  solar  farm  shall  decommission  and 

remove  the  farm  when  the  farm  is  at  the  end  of  its  useful  life.  A 

solar  farm  is  presumed  to  be  at  the  end  of  its  useful  life  when 

the  farm  generates  no  electricity  for  a  continuous  360-day 

period.  This  presumption  may  be  rebutted  by  an  owner  or  operator

resume  operation  or  the  farm's  existence  will  serve  some 

educational  or  research  purpose.  In  such  cases  of  educational  or 

research  purpose,  the  owner  or  operator's  obligation  to 

decommission  may  be  extended  by  the  Commission  until  the  solar 

farm's  educational  and  research  purpose  is  complete.  Decommission 

requirements  are  described  in  the  act.
