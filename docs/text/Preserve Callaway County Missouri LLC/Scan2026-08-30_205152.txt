lll.  JUSTIFICATION  FOR  PROPOSED  TERMS 

Crusoe  respectfully  submits  that  the  proposed  25-year  term  and  85%  abatement  are 
well-supported  by  the  specific  economic  and  development  characteristics  of  this  project  and  the 

Callaway  County  market.  The  following  factors,  taken  together, distinguish  this  project  from  sites 

in  competing  jurisdictions: 

1.  Higher  Property Tax Rate  Than  Comparable  Sites 

Callaway  County’s  effective  property  tax  rate  on  commercial  and  industrial  real  and  personal 

property 

is  materially  higher 

than 

that  of  comparable  data  center  sites  under  active 

consideration  in  Missouri  and  the  nation.  The  85%  abatement  corrects  for  this  structural  cost 

disparity  and  places  the  County  on  an  equal  footing  with  competing  sites. 

2.  Hyperscaler Tenant Requirements  &  Site  Competitiveness 

Crusoe  is  pursuing  a  hyperscaler  tenant  for  this  facility.  The  tenant  will  be  a  large-scale, 

credit-worthy  technology  company  with  the  financial  capacity  to  make  substantial  ongoing 

investments  in  GPU  and  computing  infrastructure  at  the  site.  Hyperscaler  tenants  operate  under 

rigorous 

internal 

return-on-capital 

frameworks  and  evaluate 

competing 

sites  on 

a 

total-cost-of-ownership  basis.  An  abatement  structure  that  keeps  the  all-in  occupancy  cost 

competitive  is  often  a  threshold  requirement  for  site  selection.  Securing  such  a  tenant  is  critical 

not  only  to  the  viability  of  this  project,  but  to  maximizing  the  long-term  capital  investment  and 

associated  economic  activity  in  Callaway  County. 

3.  Higher  Utility  &  Transmission Costs 

Data  center  operations  are  energy-intensive.  The  utility  and  transmission  cost  structure  for  this 

prospective  project  is  higher  than  in  certain  competing  markets,  adding  meaningful  operating 

cost  pressure to  the  project  on  an  annual  basis. 

4.  Higher  Public  Improvement  Costs 

Development  of  this  site  will  require  significant  public  infrastructure  improvements.  These 

include  road  improvements  to  accommodate  the  construction  trafÏc  and  ongoing  operational 
logistics  associated  with  a  data  center  campus,  as  well  as  a  necessary  waterline  upgrade  to 
ensure  adequate  water  supply  and  pressure  for  cooling  and  fire  suppression  systems.  These 
capital  costs,  borne  directly  by  Crusoe,  represent a  real  and  material  cost to  develop the  project.

City of Holts Summit Announces  Retirement of City Administrator Brandon  Ruediger 

Holts  Summit,  Missouri,  3/12/2026 —  After  more  than  six  years  of  dedicated  service  to the  City  of  Holts 
Summit,  City Administrator Brandon  Ruediger has  announced  his  retirement from  public service, effective 
March  15,  2026. 

Ruediger joined  the  City of Holts  Summit  in  January of 2020  and  worked  closely with  the  Mayor, Board  of 
Aldermen,  city staff,  local  businesses,  and  residents to strengthen city operations,  improve infrastructure, 
and  guide  responsible  community growth. 

During  his  tenure,  the  City  of Holts  Summit  experienced  significant  progress across  multiple  areas  of city 
government.  Key  accomplishments  during  this  time  include  strengthening  the  city’s  financial  stability 
through  responsible  budgeting  and  long-term  financial  planning,  investing  in  critical  infrastructure 
improvements,  enhancing  public  safety  operations  and  equipment,  modernizing  internal  administrative 
systems,  and  improving transparency and  communication  with  residents. 

Under  Ruediger’s  leadership,  the  city  also  focused  on  improving operational  efÏciency  and  strengthening 

internal  policies to  better support employees and  ensure effective delivery of services to  residents.  Efforts 

were  made  to  modernize  technology  systems,  improve  fleet  and  facility  management  practices,  and 

expand  safety  and  risk  management  initiatives for city  departments. 

Ruediger  also  worked  to  strengthen  partnerships  throughout  the  region  and  support  the  continued 

growth  of  the  local  business  community.  By  fostering  collaboration  with  local  organizations,  business 

leaders,  and  community  groups,  the  city  continued  to  build  momentum  as  an  attractive  and  growing 

community within  Callaway  County. 

Reflecting  on  his  time  with  the  city,  Ruediger  expressed  deep  appreciation  for  the  people  who  helped 

move the  community forward. 

“Serving  the  City  of  Holts  Summit  has  been  one  of the  greatest  honors  of  my  professional  career,” said 

Ruediger. “|  want to  sincerely thank the  Mayor and  the  Board  of Aldermen  for their leadership,  trust,  and 

support  throughout  the  years.  Their  commitment  to  this  community  has  played  a  major  role  in  the 

progress the  city  has  achieved.” 

Ruediger also  recognized the  dedication  of city  employees who  serve  the  community  every day.
