Good afternoon, 

I would like to  share my concerns regarding the proposed data center development 
in Callaway County.  Although I understand the  importance of attracting  new 
business  and  investment,  I  do not believe this project is  the  right fit for our 
community in  its  current form. 

There  are several issues that I  feel deserve  closer attention before any approvals 
move  forward: 

1.  Environmental Concerns —  Large-scale data  centers  can  consume  enormous 

amounts of water and  create  ongoing noise from cooling equipment  and backup 
generators,  which may negatively affect nearby residents and wildlife. 
2.  Energy Demand —  The  substantial electricity requirements associated with 
these  facilities could place added strain on the power grid and potentially 
increase  utility costs  for local households. 

3.  Community and Land Impact —  Callaway County’s rural character and 

agricultural  landscape  are important to  many residents.  A project of this  scale 
could permanently  alter the  area and  disrupt farmland and natural  habitats. 

While tax revenue and economic  growth are often cited as  benefits,  developments of 
this magnitude  should be held to  strict standards  to  ensure  they do  not negatively 
impact surrounding communities. 

At a minimum,  I  encourage  the commission to consider the  following safeguards: 

e  Strong noise-control requirements to  limit disturbance to  neighboring 

properties. 

¢  Restrictions  against construction in flood-prone areas or locations with 

sensitive water resources. 

¢  Oversight of water and  power consumption to  protect availability and 

affordability for local  residents. 

e  An independent  study examining possible heat-related effects on  nearby 

farmland and agriculture. 

I respectfully ask the  commission to  either deny this proposal or delay consideration 
until  a  full  environmental  and community impact review can be completed. 

Thank you for your time and consideration.
