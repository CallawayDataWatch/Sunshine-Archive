As for a  public  meeting, that  is  exactly why the  Chamber of Commerce  and  Crusoe  put  on  the open 
house  event.  Because that event was  not a  commission-sponsored  meeting does  not  mean that the 
commission  is  operating in  private.  The  commission  has  been  very open  to  meet for discussions  of 
public  concerns.  Two  recent such  meetings (Monday 7/27  and  Wednesday 7/29)  were  recorded  as 
ofÏcial  Commission  business,  and those  recordings are  available from the  County Clerk website along. 

with  all  other Commission  meetings. 

https://callawaycountyclerk.com/?page_id=20969 

The  idea  that the  County  MUST  hold  a  meeting similar to the  public open  house  already held  seems to 

indicate that the  public would treat the same  information  to a  greater degree  if it  were  delivered  by the 
Commission,  rather than from  the  developer and the  local Chamber of Commerce.  On  that point,  | 

believe that we will  have to  agree to  disagree.  Folks are going to tend  to trust  only the  information  with 
which they agree  most,  not the  same  information delivered form a  different source.  The  information  is 
the  information...  and  the  best  information  is  that delivered  from  the  developer... which  has  been 

publicly delivered  and  reported  by  many  local  media  outlets. 

The  Commission  has  been  and  will  continue to  be  open for public discussions.  As  always,  if you  would
