Development

Dear Commissioner Jungermann  and  all  the  people  addressed  in  this  message, 

As  a  resident  and  taxpayer of Callaway County,  |  am  writing  to you  directly to  express  my  deep 

concerns  and  formal  opposition to  the  proposed  data  center project  in  our  county,  unless  stringent, 

legally  binding  protections  for  our community  are  established  first. 

While  economic  growth  is  important,  it  should  never  come  at the  expense  of the  people  who  live  here, 

pay taxes  here,  and  tend  to  this  land.  Al  data  centers  are  not  standard  commercial  developments; they 

are  massive  industrial  operations  disguised  as tech  ofÏces.  |  urge you  to  look  beyond  the  promised  tax 

revenues and  weigh  the  permanent,  irreversible impacts this  facility  will  have  on  our  county. 
|  ask that you  carefully consider the following  critical  impacts  before  moving  forward: 

Erosion  of Our  Rural  Heritage  and  Beautiful  Land:  Callaway County  is  defined  by  its  agricultural 

strength,  open  spaces,  and  natural  beauty.  Dropping  a  massive,  industrial  data  center complex into our 

landscape  fundamentally disrupts the  rural  character of our  area,  harms  local  wildlife  corridors,  and 

fragments  productive agricultural  land.  Once this  beautiful  land  is  cleared  and  industrialized, we  can 

never get  it  back. 

The  Burden  on  Local  Taxpayers: We  need  concrete guarantees that  local  residents  will  not  foot the  bill 

for the  immense  infrastructure  upgrades  these  facilities  require. The  massive  energy  and  water 

demands  of data  centers  frequently  lead  to  rising  utility  rates  for  local  citizens  and  put  an 

unprecedented  strain  on  our electric  grid.  Taxpayers should  not  see  their  hard-earned  money used to 

subsidize  corporate utilities  while  risking  blackouts  or rate  hikes. 

Severe  Environmental  and  Resource  Strain:  The  cooling  systems  required  for these  centers  consume 

millions  of gallons  of water daily,  threatening  our  local  water tables.  Furthermore,  the  constant,  low- 

frequency hum  of  industrial  chillers  and  massive  backup  generators  creates  a  permanent  radius  of 

noise  pollution  that  shatters the  peace  of rural  residential  life. 

As  our  Presiding  Commissioner, your first duty  is  to  safeguard  the well-being  of Callaway  County's 

citizens  and  land.  At  a  bare  minimum,  |  urge  the  Commission  to  institute  an  immediate  moratorium  on 

approvals to  conduct  an  independent,  comprehensive  impact study. We  must  mandate  strict  property- 

line  noise  mitigation,  prohibit  building  in  sensitive environmental  areas  or  floodplains,  and  require 

thermal  pollution  studies to  ensure these facilities  do  not create  localized  "heat  domes"  that damage 

surrounding  crops. 

The taxpayers  of Callaway County  are watching  this  decision  closely. We expect our  elected  leaders to 

protect our  resources,  our  pocketbooks,  and  our way  of life. 

Thank you  for your time,  your  leadership,  and  your attention  to this  critical  matter. |  look forward  to 

your  response. 

Sincerely,
