Some  of  my  primary  concerns  include: 

1.  Environmental  Impact —  The  potential  for excessive water usage,  increased  noise  pollution from 

cooling  systems  and  generators,  and  other  environmental  strain  on  surrounding  areas. 

2.  Utility  and  Grid  Reliability  - The  substantial  energy demands  of a  large-scale  data  center could  place 

added  pressure on  local  infrastructure and  potentially  increase  utility costs  for  residents. 

3.  Land  Use  and  Community  Character —  This  type  of industrial  development does  not  align  with  the 

rural  and  residential  nature  of our  area.  It  may  negatively  impact  agricultural  land,  wildlife  habitats,  and 

the  overall  character  of the  community. 

While  projects  like  this  may generate tax  revenue,  they are  also  intensive  industrial  operations that 

require strong  oversight and  regulation  to  ensure  they do  not  negatively affect  surrounding 

communities.  At  a  minimum,  |  urge the  commission  to consider the following  safeguards  when 

evaluating  any future  proposals: 

*  Requiring  effective  noise  mitigation  measures to  ensure  operations  remain  within  acceptable  limits  at 

surrounding  property lines. 

*  Protecting  environmentally sensitive  areas  by  prohibiting construction  in  floodplains  or  regions with 

high  water tables. 

*  Regulating  water and  electrical  usage  to  ensure  local  residents  are  not  impacted  by increased  costs 

or  reduced  availability of essential  utilities. 

*  Conducting  a thorough study  on  potential  thermal  pollution  and the  effects  of “heat domes”  on 

nearby agriculture  and  ecosystems. 

|  respectfully urge  the commission  to  reject  this  proposal  or, at the very least,  establish  a  moratorium 

until  a  comprehensive environmental  and  community  impact study can  be  completed. 

Thank you  for your time  and  consideration.
