You  asked  about  benefits: 

The  local  benefits  of the  data  centers center around  requiring the  data  center developers to maintain  a 
minimum  number of jobs and to  pay taxes.  These two  points are highly contentious  in  public comment 
and  highly negotiable when talking with  data  center developers.  The jobs are  required to  be  maintained 

in  order to  qualify for tax abatements.  The  taxes that these data  centers  do  pay may be  used to fund 
local  school  districts,  local fire districts,  community  improvement projects,  public  protection  projects, 

and  other projects to  promote the growth and  financial  stability of the county for  many years to  come. 

Are the trade-offs worth  the  data  center presence...?  The  Commission  is  working  hard to  educate 
ourselves,  evaluate data  center construction  and  operations from around the country in  the attempt to 
determine the detailed  impacts on  our county, specifically.  In  order to  make  sound  decisions, the 
Commission  needs factual,  specific data  about our situation.  Not all  data  centers  are alike  - they  are 
constantly evolving  in  technology used  in  their construction  and  cooling systems. 

Until  Callaway-specific  project facts are  made known  - to this  Commission  and  to the  public, there  can 
be  no  meaningful  discussions that will  foster a  fertile  decision-making environment.  Until  that time, 
discussions  can  only contain generalities which  may  possibly  have  little  or  no  basis in  any  proposed 
Callaway County  project. 

Thank you  for your concern  and  for your input.

Dear  Commissioners, 

lam a  resident  of  Fulton,  MO  and  |  am  writing  to  formally express  my opposition  to  the 

proposed  (and  future  proposals  of)  data  center  project(s)  in  Callaway County. 

While  I  support  economic development,  |  believe  this  specific  project  poses  significant  risks 

to  our community. 

My concerns  include: 

1.  Environmental  Impact:  The  potential  for excessive water  consumption  and  noise  pollution 

from  cooling  systems  and  generators. 

2.  Grid  Reliability:  The  immense  energy demands  could  cause  utility  rates  to  rise  for  local 

residents. 

3.  Land  Use:  This  project does  not  align with  the  rural/residential character of our area, 

disrupting  agricultural  land  and  local  wildlife. 

While these facilities  provide tax  revenue, they are  intense  industrial  users  that  require 

careful  regulation  to  be  "good  neighbors".  At a  minimum,  please  consider the following 

when  evaluating  new  proposals: 

*  Mandating  noise  mitigation:  Restricting  chiller operations to  meet  noise  limits  at  property 

lines. 

*  Environmental  Protection:  Prohibiting  projects  in  floodplains  or  areas  with  high  water 

tables,  and  regulating  water and  electric  usage  such  that  it  does  not  impact the  availability 

or costs  of these  basic  utilities  for the  citizens  of the  surrounding  communities. 

*  Thermal  Pollution  Study:  Evaluating  the  impact  of  "heat  domes"  on  surrounding 

agriculture. 

|  urge the  commission  to  reject  this  proposal  or, at  minimum,  institute  a  moratorium  to 

conduct  a  comprehensive  impact  study.
